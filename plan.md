# Plan — Block 1 (weeks 1–4, starting 2026-11-02)

**Goal order:** fat loss → running/cardio/flexibility → keep muscle.
**Mode:** back-cautious until a professional assesses the back (see profile). No heavy back squats or deadlifts this block.

## Stop rules (read first)
- Pain going down the leg, numbness, or leg/foot weakness → stop the session, book the assessment now.
- Numbness in the groin area or any bladder/bowel control change → doctor the same day.
- Lower-back tightness that is still there the next morning → swap that day's runs/jumps for the bike, and note it in the log.

## Mobility routine (warm-up, and on rest days)
5–8 min, all gentle, never into pain:
- Cat-camel x 8
- Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, 30 s / side
- 90/90 hip switches x 8
- Open-book thoracic rotation x 8 / side
- Calf stretch against a wall, 30 s / side
- Glute bridge x 10

Skip any aggressive hamstring stretching while leg symptoms are possible.

## Nutrition
Fat loss comes mostly from the diet. Training mainly decides whether what you lose is fat or muscle.

| Target | Amount | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | ~2,400 kcal/day | Guess. Adjusted from your weight trend, not this formula |
| Protein | 170–185 g/day | Likely. Main lever for keeping muscle |
| Fibre | 30–38 g/day, raised over 2 weeks | Likely. Also for the constipation |
| Water | 3 L+/day, more on run days | Likely |

**Minimum tracking:** log everything you eat in an app for 2–4 weeks to learn your portions, then move to protein only + weigh-ins.

**Constipation:** raise fibre gradually (veg, fruit, oats, lentils/beans, 2 kiwis or a few prunes a day), drink more water as fibre rises, and keep the daily walks. See a doctor if you see blood, have severe pain, lose weight without explanation, or your bowel habits change and don't go back to normal.

## Weekly check
- Weigh 3 mornings a week (after the toilet, before eating) and put the average in `logs/body.md`.
- Measure your waist at the navel once a week.
- **Adjust after 2 full weeks, not before:**
  - Losing less than 0.3 kg/week → cut 150–200 kcal/day.
  - Losing more than 1 kg/week → add 150–200 kcal/day.
  - Between the two → change nothing.
