# Diet plan — Block 1 (from 2026-11-10)

**Targets:** ~2,300 kcal (Guess, corrected by your weight trend) · protein 170–185 g · fibre 30–38 g · water 3 L+.
**Main rule:** 4 meals a day and no long gaps. Never skip meals to make up for a big meal the day before.

## Daily structure
| Time | Meal | ~kcal | ~Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07:00 | Weigh-in, then breakfast | 400 | 40 g |
| 12:30 | Lunch at work (packed) | 550 | 45 g |
| 17:00 | Home meal (plate rule) | 600 | 40 g |
| After the 17:00 meal | One sweet | 150–200 | — |
| After the gym (~21:00) | Recovery meal | 450–500 | 45–50 g |
| **Total** | | **~2,200–2,300** | **~170–180 g** |

Black coffee and diet soft drinks are free. Ketchup, light mayo and sauces: about 1 tablespoon per meal.

## Breakfast (07:00) — pick one
| Option | What | Est. kcal / protein |
|---|---|---|
| A | 3 eggs + 1 slice wholegrain toast + 170 g Greek yogurt | ~410 / 39 g |
| B | 250 g Greek yogurt + 1 scoop whey stirred in + 30 g oats + a handful of berries or ½ banana | ~420 / 50 g |
| C (no time) | Whey shake with water + 40 g oats or 1 banana, eaten on the way | ~280 / 30 g |

## Lunch at work (12:30) — pack the night before
| Option | What | Est. kcal / protein |
|---|---|---|
| A | 150 g cooked chicken breast + 150 g cooked rice + big salad + 1 tsp olive oil | ~530 / 48 g |
| B | 1 can tuna in water (drained) + 1 wholegrain wrap + salad + 1 tbsp light mayo | ~400 / 33 g. Add a Greek yogurt to reach ~500 / 50 g |
| C | 150 g lean beef or chicken + 1 cup cooked lentils + salad | ~550 / 50 g |

## Home meal (17:00) — the plate rule for your mother's cooking
- **½ plate** vegetables or salad.
- **Protein:** 1.5 palm-sized pieces of meat or chicken (~150–200 g cooked).
- **Carbs:** rice about 1 cupped hand (~1 cup cooked), or 1 bread. Not both.
- **Fava beans, lentils or other legume dishes:** 1 bowl + 2 boiled eggs or a Greek yogurt on the side. Legumes alone are too low in protein.
- **No seconds of rice.** Seconds of vegetables or protein are fine.

## After the gym (~21:00)
| Option | What | Est. kcal / protein |
|---|---|---|
| A (what you already eat) | 150–200 g chicken breast + 2 slices wholegrain toast + salad | ~450–500 / 45–55 g |
| B (late or tired) | Whey shake + 2 slices wholegrain toast with a little cheese or labneh + 1 fruit | ~450 / 40 g |

**Rest days:** swap this meal for 250 g Greek yogurt + fruit, or a whey shake (~250 kcal / 25–40 g).

## Sweets
One planned sweet a day (~150–200 kcal): 1–2 date biscuits, a small piece of chocolate, or a small dessert. Eat it **after a meal**, never on an empty stomach.

## Eating out
One restaurant meal a week fits. On that day, keep breakfast and lunch protein-heavy, and pick either the dessert or the rice/fries, not both. **The next day is a normal day. No skipping.**

## Fibre + constipation
- Raise fibre over 2 weeks, not at once: vegetables at 3 meals, 2 fruits a day, oats, lentils/beans.
- If needed: 2 kiwis or 3–4 prunes a day.
- Water rises with fibre: finish a 1 L bottle by 12:00, one by 17:00, and one around the gym.
- See a doctor for blood, severe pain, unexplained weight loss, or a change that doesn't settle.

## Weekly prep (about 1 hour, twice a week)
- Bake or air-fry ~1 kg chicken breast. It keeps 3–4 days in the fridge.
- Cook a pot of rice. Boil 6–10 eggs.
- Keep in stock: canned tuna, Greek yogurt, whey, oats, wholegrain bread/wraps, fruit, salad vegetables.

## Tracking
- Weeks 1–2: log everything in a food app to learn your portions. App: MyFitnessPal (or FatSecret). Set 2,300 kcal and 175 g protein.
- After that: track protein only and weigh in at 07:00 three times a week.
- **Adjust only after 2 full weeks of weigh-ins** (rules in `plan.md`).
